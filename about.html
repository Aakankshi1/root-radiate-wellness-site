About the company

Root & Radiate Wellness

A wellness brand built around therapeutic yoga, mindful movement and accessible daily practices.

Our philosophy

Root & Radiate Wellness is built on a simple principle: when the body feels grounded, the mind becomes clearer and daily life becomes more balanced. The studio’s work focuses on safe, practical and structured yoga practices that can be adapted to individual needs.

The brand supports students through yoga therapy, wellness yoga, breathwork, meditation, stretching, restorative practice and personalized guidance.

What we stand for

  • Personalized attention instead of generic routines.
  • Therapeutic alignment with practical lifestyle integration.
  • Calm, consistent and sustainable wellness habits.
  • Safe progression for beginners, seniors and women across life stages.
